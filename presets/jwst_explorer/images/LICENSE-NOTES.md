## Conditions (both sites, same wording)

1. "The full image or footage credit must be presented in a clear and readable manner to all users, with the wording unaltered." Credits may not be hidden or separated from the image; online credits should keep links active.
2. Material may not be used to imply ESA/Webb or ESA/Hubble endorsement of commercial products or services.
3. If an image is significantly altered, note the changes after the credit line (e.g. "Original image by ESA/Webb, warped by NN").
4. Logos are NOT covered by CC BY 4.0 and require separate written permission. Names of individuals in a credit line may not be removed.

## Practical rule for the preset

Every slide/viewer surface that shows one of these images must render the credit line above, unaltered, visibly adjacent to the image (a persistent caption chip is fine). For the Webb-vs-Hubble slider, show both credit lines.
