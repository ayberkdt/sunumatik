# Write Turkish Slide Copy

Write Turkish that is accurate when read and natural when spoken. Prefer clarity and rhythm over institutional prose.

## Set the register

Determine audience expertise, formality, discipline, and whether the deck is speaker-led, reading-first, or a technical review. Preserve established scientific terminology and the strength of the original claim.

Read references/turkish-slide-style.md for terminology, punctuation, capitalization, numbers, units, abbreviations, and common translation problems.

## Write slide headlines

Use sentence case. Prefer a concrete claim, comparison, question, or implication that can be understood in one glance. Keep topic labels neutral when evidence does not support a conclusion.

Use active verbs when they clarify agency. Remove bureaucratic padding such as gerçekleştirilmiştir, sağlanmaktadır, söz konusu, kapsamında, hususunda, and bulunmaktadır when a shorter accurate form exists.

## Write visible copy

Prefer keywords, short clauses, compact explanations, labels, and annotated numbers. Do not paste note sentences onto the slide. Do not turn every phrase into a bullet.

Introduce an unfamiliar abbreviation once. Keep Turkish suffixes, apostrophes, decimal conventions, symbols, and SI units consistent with the chosen editorial standard.

## Preserve nuance

Distinguish gözlem, çıkarım, olasılık, varsayım, sınırlılık, and öneri. Do not translate calibrated scientific language into certainty. Avoid inflated adjectives and forced surprise.

## Return variants

When wording materially affects tone, return:

- a concise preferred headline;
- a more neutral alternative;
- a speaker-note sentence carrying the omitted nuance.

Send the result to enforce-slide-copy-density. Do not reduce font size to protect wording that can be shortened or moved to notes.
