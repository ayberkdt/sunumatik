# Density and pacing

Treat limits as review triggers, not rigid laws.

| Mode | Primary messages | Body words | Bullets | Equations | Charts | Typical time |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Speaker-led | 1 | 8–30 | 0–3 | 0–1 | 0–1 | 35–75 s |
| Reading-first | 1 | 20–50 | 0–4 | 0–2 | 0–2 | 50–100 s |
| Technical-review | 1 | 20–55 | 0–5 | 1–3 | 0–2 | 60–150 s |

## Split instead of shrink

Split a slide when the title exceeds two lines, visible copy begins to read like a paragraph, two visuals compete, a derivation needs multiple conceptual steps, chart labels become smaller than body captions, or a table cannot be read at normal presentation distance. Move reasoning and caveats to notes before considering smaller type.

## Speaker notes

Use this structure:

```yaml
opening_sentence: ""
explanation: ""
evidence_cue: ""
limitation: ""
transition: ""
estimated_seconds: 55
```

Do not duplicate slide text verbatim in notes.
