# Write English Slide Copy

Write precise English for an audience listening and looking at the same time. Do not reproduce journal prose on slides.

## Set the register

Determine audience expertise, venue, discipline, formality, and density profile. Preserve domain terminology, uncertainty, causality, and evidentiary strength.

Read references/english-slide-style.md for sentence case, articles, verbs, parallelism, numbers, units, abbreviations, and Turkish-to-English interference.

## Write slide headlines

Prefer concrete claims, comparisons, questions, or implications. Use a neutral topic heading when the result remains unresolved. Keep headlines scannable and usually within one or two lines.

Use active voice when agency matters, but do not force it. Remove paper-like scaffolding such as it can clearly be seen that, it should be noted that, in the scope of this study, and due to the fact that.

## Write visible copy

Use short clauses, key phrases, labels, and annotated values. Put reasoning, caveats, and transitions in speaker notes. Avoid noun stacks, unexplained abbreviations, and bullets that are complete paragraphs.

## Preserve scientific calibration

Distinguish observed, consistent with, suggests, supports, predicts, estimates, and demonstrates. Do not replace cautious findings with certainty or add dramatic adjectives.

## Return variants

When useful, provide a concise preferred headline, a conservative alternative, and a speaker-note sentence that carries necessary nuance.

Send the result to enforce-slide-copy-density. Do not solve crowding with smaller typography.
