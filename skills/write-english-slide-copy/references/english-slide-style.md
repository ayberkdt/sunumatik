# English slide style

## Headlines

Use sentence case. Prefer a claim or meaningful relationship:

| Paper-like | Presentation-ready |
|---|---|
| Analysis of atmospheric retrieval results | Clouds limit the retrieval below 2 μm |
| It can clearly be seen that error decreases | Error falls as calibration improves |
| Results obtained within the scope of the study | The test excludes two failure modes |
| A comparison of the two methods | Method B trades speed for stability |

Use topic headings when the evidence is exploratory: Retrieval uncertainty, not Clouds explain the spectrum.

## Turkish-to-English interference

Avoid literal forms such as:

- in the scope of this study;
- an increase has been experienced;
- information about the subject;
- making an analysis;
- it was provided that;
- as can be understood from the figure.

Choose a direct verb and name the actor or measured quantity.

## Scientific calibration

- observed: directly measured or recorded;
- consistent with: compatible but not uniquely established;
- suggests: weak or indirect directional evidence;
- supports: evidence favors the claim;
- predicts: output of a model or theory;
- estimates: inferred quantity with uncertainty;
- demonstrates: reserve for strong direct evidence.

Preserve may, could, under these assumptions, within uncertainty, and other scope conditions.

## Mechanics

Keep abbreviations, capitalization, hyphenation, numbers, SI units, and serial punctuation consistent with the selected house style. Avoid dense noun stacks; unpack the relationship with a verb.

Put complete reasoning and transitions in notes. Use visible copy for the claim, evidence anchor, comparison, or labeled visual.
